## What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device to help websites operate, remember preferences, and understand usage patterns.

SiteSpresso may also use related technologies for session handling and performance monitoring.

## Cookie Categories We Use

- **Strictly necessary:** authentication/session cookies, security, and core product functionality (sign-in, dashboard, editor). These are required for the service to work.
- **Performance / analytics:** product analytics and speed insights (for example Vercel Analytics and Speed Insights) used to understand reliability and performance.
- **Functional (if enabled):** preferences that improve the product experience beyond the bare minimum.

We do not use advertising cookies on the core SiteSpresso product pages as of this draft. If that changes, this policy will be updated.

## Third-Party Services

Some cookies or similar storage may be set by integrated services such as hosting (Vercel), authentication/database (Supabase), billing (Stripe Checkout / Customer Portal), and analytics providers.

Those providers process data according to their own documentation and their contracts with SiteSpresso.

## How to Manage Cookies

Most browsers let you block or delete cookies. Blocking strictly necessary cookies may prevent sign-in and other essential features from working.
