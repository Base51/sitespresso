## Data We Collect

Depending on how you use the service, we may process:

- **Account data:** email address, authentication identifiers, profile settings (for example display name and plan).
- **Billing metadata:** Stripe customer and subscription IDs, plan, billing interval, and invoice references. Payment card details are processed by Stripe; we do not store full card numbers.
- **Site and generation inputs:** business name, location, industry, prompts, generated drafts, edits, logos/images you upload, and published site content.
- **Usage and diagnostics:** product events, approximate usage metrics, error logs, and security signals needed to operate and protect the platform.
- **Communications:** messages you send to support or legal inboxes.

## How We Use Data

We use personal data to:

- Provide authentication, generation, editing, publishing, custom domains, and account features.
- Manage subscriptions, entitlements, and abuse prevention.
- Improve generation quality and reliability (including aggregated or de-identified analysis where appropriate).
- Meet legal, tax, and accounting obligations.
- Respond to privacy and support requests.

Where GDPR or similar laws apply, we rely on one or more of: performance of a contract, legitimate interests (secure and improve the service), consent (where required), and legal obligation.

## Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object, or request portability of your personal data, and to withdraw consent where processing is consent-based.
