**Last updated (proposed):** 2026-09-28

The SiteSpresso Data Processing Addendum (DPA) sets out data-protection terms for customers that need a processor agreement with **[LEGAL_ENTITY_NAME]** ("SiteSpresso").

## Purpose and Applicability

This DPA is intended for customers that process personal data under laws that require a data processing agreement (for example GDPR Art. 28).

Request a signed DPA by emailing legal@sitespresso.com with your account email and legal entity details.

## Roles

- **Customer** is typically the controller for personal data in site content and end-user submissions on published sites.
- **SiteSpresso** acts as processor when processing that data to host, generate, edit, and publish sites on the customer's behalf.
- For SiteSpresso's own account, billing, and product analytics data, SiteSpresso acts as controller (see the Privacy Policy).

## Subprocessors

SiteSpresso uses subprocessors for hosting, authentication/database, payments, and AI infrastructure, including Supabase, Vercel, Stripe, and OpenAI.

A current subprocessor list is available on request via legal@sitespresso.com. Material changes will be communicated as required by the signed DPA.

## Security and Incidents

SiteSpresso applies technical and organizational measures designed to protect personal data.

Security incidents are evaluated under internal response processes and contractual notice commitments in the signed DPA.

## How to Request a DPA

Email legal@sitespresso.com with:

- Account email
- Legal entity name and address
- Any customer template or questionnaire you need us to review

We will send the current DPA template and next steps for review and signature.

Questions? See `/legal/contact`.
